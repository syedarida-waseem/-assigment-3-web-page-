HISTORICAL HERITAGE · LAHORE

The Lahore Fort

Lahore Fort is one of Pakistan's most important historical monuments. Located in the Walled City of Lahore, it reflects the architectural beauty and royal traditions of the Mughal period.

The fort contains remarkable structures, decorated halls, impressive gates and artistic details. Along with the nearby Shalimar Gardens, it was recognized as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1981.
