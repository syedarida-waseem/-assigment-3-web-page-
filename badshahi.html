HISTORICAL HERITAGE · LAHORE

Badshahi Mosque

Badshahi Mosque is one of Lahore's most famous historical landmarks. It was commissioned by Mughal Emperor Aurangzeb and completed in 1673. Its grand domes and red sandstone architecture make it a remarkable example of Mughal design.

The mosque stands near Lahore Fort and is an important symbol of Pakistan's cultural and architectural heritage. Its spacious courtyard, beautiful marble details and towering minarets continue to attract visitors from around the world.
