THE LEGACY OF LAHORE

Lahore is one of Pakistan’s most historic cities, known for its magnificent architecture, rich cultural heritage, and remarkable landmarks. From the grand Lahore Fort and the beautiful Badshahi Mosque to the historic Wazir Khan Mosque and Shalimar Gardens, the city reflects centuries of Mughal and traditional influence. Its ancient streets, detailed artwork, and impressive monuments tell stories of powerful rulers, artistic achievements, and cultural traditions. Today, these historical places continue to attract visitors and preserve Lahore’s identity as a city where history and beauty exist together.
