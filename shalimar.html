HISTORICAL HERITAGE · LAHORE

Shalimar Gardens

Shalimar Gardens is one of Lahore's most beautiful historical landmarks. Built during the Mughal era under Emperor Shah Jahan in 1641–1642, the gardens represent the Mughal love for nature, symmetry and elegant design.

The gardens are famous for their three descending terraces, flowing water channels, fountains and green surroundings. Their carefully planned layout reflects the creativity and engineering skills of Mughal builders.

Along with Lahore Fort, Shalimar Gardens became a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1981. Today, they remain an important part of Lahore's cultural and architectural heritage.
